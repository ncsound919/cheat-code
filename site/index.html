Who it's for

For the bettor who keeps receipts.

Not a tout. Not a lock shop. A lab for people who want to know why a number moved, whether the edge survived a season, and what happened the last twenty times it didn't.

It fits if
  • You already track your bets, even badly.
  • You care whether an edge survives a full season.
  • You want the model's reasoning, not just its confidence.
It doesn't if
  • You want a lock, a guarantee or a shortcut.
  • You want someone else to make the call.
  • You are looking for a way around sample size.

Questions

The honest answers.

No odds guarantees, no “can't lose”, no pretending a paper bet is a wager.

Does it place bets?

No. Cheat Code is paper trading and analysis. The parlay cards can build a shareable bet slip for a tracker such as Gambly or Pikkit, but the app cannot fill or place a wager, hold funds, or connect to a sportsbook account — and no sportsbook publishes a public prefill for another app.

Where does the data come from?

Two read-only providers you configure: one for prop prices, one for box scores. A sync writes the board and settles finished games. Screenshots are read on the phone and never uploaded. After a fantasy capture, only player names are sent to ESPN's public directory for headshots and club logos.

What do I need before I start?

An Android 10+ phone, a prop-price key and a box-score key. Before those are added, the app says it is running on seeded examples. It does not pretend synthetic data is live.

Does it work offline?

The board needs a sync. The model, the sandbox, the manual and the cards work on the phone with no network once the data is there.

Do I need an account?

No. Sign-in is optional and exists only to carry strategies between devices. It uses email one-time codes, with no password to store.

What does Premium add?

Ten-leg parlays, the fantasy desk with lineup capture and weekly write-ups, the sports wire, watermark-free share cards and a cloud-model fallback. It does not unlock a stronger formula, a better line or a different gate.

Can I run my own model?

Yes. The model shop reads this phone's memory and storage, recommends a size, then downloads a GGUF straight from Hugging Face. Weights stay in the app's private storage. You can also point it at any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

Is this gambling advice?

No. It is a probability ledger. A positive expected value is an estimate, not a guarantee, and a parlay multiplies the book's margin along with the payout. If gambling stops being fun, call 1-800-522-4700.
